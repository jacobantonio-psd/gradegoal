How it works:
Midterm initial grade = Class standing × 60% + Midterm exam % × 40%

Finals initial grade = Class standing × 50% + Final exam % × 50%

Raw grade = (Midterm initial + Finals initial) ÷ 2

Final grade = Raw grade looked up in the transmutation table (the highest raw value that is at or below your raw grade, without rounding first).

With a lab: lecture and lab are each worked out the same way, averaged across midterm and finals, then combined as Lecture × 60% + Lab × 40% before transmuting.

How it works:
GWA = Σ (Grade × Units) ÷ Σ Units

Each subject's grade is weighted by its units, so higher-unit subjects count for more. Rows with no units or no grade are left out of the total.
